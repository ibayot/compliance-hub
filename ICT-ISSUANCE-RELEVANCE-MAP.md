This document explains why each seeded issuance in the Compliance Hub is ICT-relevant for operations, governance, and safety.

## Inclusion Rule
Include all issuances and standards directly used in ICT operations, governance, security/cybersecurity, business continuity, disaster response, online safety, procurement controls, and records/compliance management.
